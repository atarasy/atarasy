# Native digital choices

The authenticated digital approval view now allows an explicit unsent choice for each offered candidate. No choice is preselected. Keep means keep for this household; onward gifting is not added. Maker, carrier, alternatives, argument against and applicable disclosures remain beside each choice.

The draft computes selected goods, supplied carriage and total only after every candidate has a choice. Gift goods contribute zero. Unknown carriage, expired offer or mandate, a non-offered candidate, or arithmetic beyond the protocol's exact integer range prevents a total. Currency is not inferred. Refresh, leaving the screen and session change discard the draft. Discard choices resets every candidate to undecided.

This implements the editing portion of IOS-B15 / IOS-04–06. It does not complete that ticket. There is no signing, operation creation, dispatch, payment or offline queue. The member service currently exposes prepared physical statements and mandates; a digital decision needs its own account-scoped preparation, immutable reviewed terms, contextual assertion, atomic persisted engine effect and original-operation readback before submission is enabled. Do not route this screen to the reference engine's unauthenticated decision endpoint.
